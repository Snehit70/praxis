## Term Calendar Model

IITM BS runs three terms per year:

| Term | Usual quiz/end-term pattern | UI label |
|---|---|---|
| January term | Quiz 1 around Feb/Mar, Quiz 2 around Apr, End Term around May | `January 2026` |
| May term | Quiz 1 around Jun/Jul, Quiz 2 around Aug, End Term around Sep | `May 2026` |
| September term | Quiz 1 around Oct, Quiz 2 around Nov/Dec, End Term sometimes Dec/Jan | `September 2026` |

Recommended date-to-term inference:

| Paper date month | Inferred term |
|---|---|
| February-May | January term of same year |
| June-September | May term of same year |
| October-December | September term of same year |
| January | September term of previous year |

This is a display/grouping inference. The raw paper date and original fields must remain stored unchanged.

## Paper Bundle Model

The source website presents papers as a two-step hierarchy:

```text
Course
  Exam type
    Term sitting / paper date
      QP Bundle
        Paper variants
          Actual test
```

In CT Quiz 1, for example:

```text
CT / Quiz 1
  September 2025
    26 October 2025
      QP Bundle 31
        QIO2, QIM4, QIM3, QIM2, QID4, QID3, QID2, QDF4, QDF3, QDF2
```

The “bundle” is not the final test. It is a container for multiple variants from the same sitting. Variants may share many questions, differ in ordering, or have small data/value changes.

Recommended bundle key:

```text
exam_uuid + canonical_course_uuid + inferred_term_id + parsed_paper_date + group_id
```

Use `group_id` as a display cue (`QP Bundle 31`), but do not rely on it alone. CT shows historical cases where `group_id = 1` spans multiple dates and terms, so `group_id` alone is not globally meaningful.

## Database Implications

Keep the current raw-paper storage, but add derived structure instead of baking all inference into the base table.

Recommended additions:

### `academic_terms`

```text
id: jan-2026 / may-2026 / sep-2026
year
term_code: jan | may | sep
display_name
starts_at nullable
ends_at nullable
```

### `paper_sittings` or `paper_bundles`

```text
id
exam_uuid
course_uuid
term_id nullable
paper_date nullable
date_precision: day | month | unknown
group_id nullable
display_name: QP Bundle 31
source_confidence: exact | inferred | dirty
warning nullable
```

### `paper_variants`

Keep existing row-per-paper model, but connect each row to a bundle:

```text
bundle_id nullable
source_uuid
source_numeric_id nullable
paper_code nullable
paper_name
paper_description
year nullable
```

Important schema changes:

- Allow nullable `year`, or add `inferred_year` separately.
- Store parsed `paper_code` such as `QDF2`, `QIO2`, `QPQ1`.
- Store original numeric source IDs when available (`CODE: 1383` in the source UI).
- Keep raw name/description untouched for auditability.

### `course_assessment_profiles`

Represent course capability rather than assuming all routes show Quiz 1, Quiz 2, End Term, OPPE.

```text
course_uuid or canonical_course_name
has_quiz1
has_quiz2
has_end_term
has_oppe
oppe_count
has_roe
has_bpt
has_project
notes
source
```

This lets the UI hide impossible sections, show OPPE/project-aware pages, and avoid empty or misleading filters.

## UI Implications

The source-compatible course page should be bundle-first:

```text
Quiz 1 - Question Papers

Select Course: CT
Year: All

QP Bundle 35
15 March 2026
January 2026

QP Bundle 31
26 Oct 25
September 2025
10 variants
```

Clicking a bundle should show the variants:

```text
Go Back

CODE: 1383
Question Paper 1383
2025 Oct26: IIT M AN EXAM QIO2
Take Test
```

For our UI, the best navigation is:

```text
/exam/:examId/course/:courseId
  Bundle grid

/exam/:examId/course/:courseId/bundle/:bundleId
  Variant grid

/paper/:paperId
  Test-taking page
```
